Matcha Frappe Shake

A creamy and refreshing homemade matcha drink with a smooth texture and sweet finish.

01 — WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

  • 1–2 teaspoons matcha powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1–2 tablespoons sugar or sweetener
  • 1 cup ice cubes
  • 1 scoop vanilla ice cream (optional)
  • Whipped cream (optional)

02 — LET'S MAKE IT

Procedure

  1. Mix the matcha powder with a little warm water until smooth.
  2. Put the matcha mixture, milk, sweetener, and ice into a blender.
  3. Add vanilla ice cream if you prefer a creamier drink.
  4. Blend until smooth and frothy.
  5. Pour into a glass and add whipped cream if desired.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
